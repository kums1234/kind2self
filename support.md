## Common topics

### Permissions

Kind2Self asks for two iOS permissions:

- **Microphone** — used to record affirmations in your own voice and to capture voice notes for small wins.
- **Speech Recognition** — used to transcribe your voice notes **on-device**. Audio never leaves your phone.

If you previously denied a permission and want to enable it, open the iOS **Settings** app → scroll to **Kind2Self** → toggle the permission back on.

### iCloud Drive sync

By default, your affirmations, voice notes, and small wins are stored on your device only. If you want them to follow you across devices, open **Settings** inside Kind2Self and enable **iCloud Drive sync**. Your data is then saved to *your* iCloud Drive, in a folder named "Kind2Self," visible in the Files app.

Turning sync off moves new data back to local storage. Existing iCloud copies stay in your iCloud Drive until you remove them in the Files app.

### Favorites

You can favourite up to **5 affirmations** at any time. Once you hit the limit, tapping the heart on a new one shows a prompt — remove one of your existing favourites first. Favourites always appear at the top of your morning rotation, even if you've disabled their theme.

### Daily rotation

- **Before you've favourited anything:** the morning shows **5 affirmations** drawn from your enabled themes, so you have plenty to choose from.
- **Once you have favourites:** the morning shows your favourites plus **2 fresh picks** from themes adjacent to what you've hearted.

Picks are stable through the day and shuffle the next day.

### Notifications

Three reminders are scheduled by default and can be turned off in **Settings → Notifications**:

- **Morning** (default 5:00 AM) — a gentle nudge to read your affirmations.
- **Evening** (default 7:00 PM) — a reminder to capture one small win.
- **Weekly digest** — a summary of the past week's wins.

You can change the morning/evening times from the same screen.

### Voice transcription

When you save a voice win, Kind2Self transcribes it on-device using Apple's Speech framework so you can read it back later in **Insights**. Audio and transcripts both stay on your phone.

If a transcript doesn't appear, check that Speech Recognition is enabled in iOS Settings → Kind2Self.

### Deleting your data

- **One item:** swipe left on it in the Wins or Library lists.
- **All app data on this device:** delete the app from your iPhone. iOS removes the app's storage automatically.
- **iCloud copies (if you enabled sync):** open the Files app → iCloud Drive → Kind2Self → delete the folder.

There is no server-side copy because Kind2Self has no server.
